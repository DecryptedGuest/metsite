About SCO-19
SCO-19 is the Metropolitan Police Service's Specialist Firearms Command — the unit's authorised firearms officers. Your rank and standing below are read directly from the SCO-19 Roblox group.

Firearms authorisation and unit progression are handled in-server and through the group ranks; there is no tryout process to sign up for here.
