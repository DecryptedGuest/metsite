MET Database Sync

Compares the MET database against the MET Roblox group. Anyone no longer in the group is removed, and newly joined constables are written into the rows that frees up — with their Discord ID filled in where it can be resolved. Timezone, WTBT and anything else the sheet carries are left blank rather than guessed at. Always run Check first: it shows exactly what will change before anything is written.

MET Database Audit

Checks every rank tab of the MET database against the MET Roblox group: who is on the wrong tab, who is in the group but on no tab, who is on the sheet but has left, duplicate rows, blank day cells and anything in a day column that isn't a number. Fill blanks writes 0 into empty or unreadable day cells. Reset all points zeroes every day cell so counting starts clean — the weekly quota is 3 events, one database point each. Neither ever touches a cell marked EX or LOA, and neither adds, deletes or moves a row: rank-tab moves are listed for you to make.
